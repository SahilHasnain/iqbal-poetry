Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Allama Iqbal Poetry is a poetry reader app that presents Urdu poetry and its Roman/transliteration form to users. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or login of any kind.

On-Device Content

All poetry content is bundled with the app and stored on your device. The app works fully offline and does not upload your reading activity or content to any server.

Local Storage Only

The app stores a small amount of information locally on your device to improve your experience, including:

  • Your preferred language (Urdu or Roman)
  • The book you last opened
  • Kalams you have marked as favorites

This information never leaves your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

No Tracking or Advertising

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and no third-party SDKs that monitor your behavior.

No Third-Party Sharing

Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to share, sell, or transfer to any third party.

Children's Privacy

The app collects no personal information from any user, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
